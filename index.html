1. Introduction

Welcome to Cricket Quick Score, a free offline cricket scoring application developed by MD Minhaj Rahman. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information when you use our app.

By using Cricket Quick Score, you agree to the terms described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the app.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide: The app allows you to enter player names, team names, and match scores. This data is stored only on your device using a local SQLite database and is never transmitted to any server.

Information collected automatically (via AdMob):

  • Device Advertising ID (used for ad personalization)
  • IP address (used to determine approximate location for ads)
  • Device information (model, OS version, screen size)
  • App usage data (interactions with ads)
  • Diagnostic data (crash reports via Firebase, if applicable)
ℹ️ Note: Cricket Quick Score itself does not collect, store, or share any of the above data. This data is collected solely by Google AdMob under Google's own Privacy Policy.

3. Google AdMob & Advertising

This app uses Google AdMob (provided by Google LLC) to display interstitial advertisements. AdMob uses the Advertising ID on your device to serve personalized or non-personalized ads.

AdMob may collect and process the following data for advertising purposes:

  • Advertising ID / GAID (Google Advertising Identifier)
  • General device & network information
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalization"
  • Or visit: https://www.google.com/settings/ads

Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

AdMob's Terms: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

You can learn more about how Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services by visiting: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. Data Storage & Retention

All match data (scores, player names, match history) entered by users is stored locally on the device using SQLite. This data is never uploaded to any external server.

Retention: Data remains on your device until you manually delete it within the app (via Settings → Clear History) or uninstall the application.

Security: We implement standard Android security practices to protect locally stored data. However, we cannot guarantee absolute security of data stored on a device that has been rooted or compromised.

5. App Permissions

Cricket Quick Score requests the following permission:

  • INTERNET — Required only for loading advertisements via Google AdMob. The app does not use this permission to upload any user data.

No other sensitive permissions (camera, contacts, location, microphone, storage) are requested.

7. GDPR — Rights of EU/EEA Users

If you are located in the European Union or European Economic Area, you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of data held about you.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Right to Restriction: Request restriction of processing of your data.
  • Right to Object: Object to processing of your data for direct marketing purposes.
  • Right to Opt Out of Personalized Ads: Disable ad personalization via your device settings.
  • Right to Withdraw/Modify Consent: You can review or change your ad-consent choices at any time from within the app via Settings → Privacy Options.

Since Cricket Quick Score does not collect personal data directly, requests related to AdMob data should be directed to Google at: Google Privacy Support

To exercise your rights, contact us at: mdminhajrahman28@gmail.com
