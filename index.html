1. Introduction

Cricket Quick Score (“the app”) is a free cricket scoring app that works offline. It lets you score matches ball by ball, view scorecards and charts, and keep a history of matches on your own device. It is developed by MD Minhaj Rahman (“we”, “us”), based in Bangladesh.

This policy explains what information the app handles, what third parties collect when you use it, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please stop using the app and uninstall it.

2. Information you enter

You can type team names, player names and match settings (overs, toss, batting or bowling choice), and you score the match ball by ball. The app saves this together with the resulting scores, wickets, extras, over-by-over history, partnerships and fall-of-wickets lists.

  • This data is saved only in a local SQLite database on your device.
  • It is never sent to us or to any server by the app.
  • Please avoid entering anything sensitive in name fields. Use nicknames or first names if you prefer.

The app also keeps two small settings on your device: your “Keep screen on” choice and a marker that records whether an ad has already been shown during an innings. Neither contains personal information.

3. Information collected automatically

The app itself does not collect, store or share personal information with us. However, the app includes Google’s advertising and consent software (Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK and the Google User Messaging Platform). When ads are loaded, Google may automatically collect the following, under Google’s own privacy policy:

DataWhy Google collects it
Advertising ID (a resettable identifier on your device)To serve and measure ads, and to personalize them where you have allowed it
IP address and approximate location derived from itTo serve ads for your region, and to prevent fraud
Device and app information (device model, operating system version, screen size, language, app name and version)To deliver ads that fit your device and to keep the service secure

We do not receive this data and we cannot identify you from it. You can read how Google handles it at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

4. Ads and your consent choices

The app is free and is supported by ads. It shows full-screen (interstitial) ads from Google AdMob, only occasionally and at a natural pause: at most once per innings, after an over has finished.

Consent

When you first open the app, Google’s User Messaging Platform may ask for your consent before ads are requested. This is shown where the law requires it, for example in the EEA and the UK. If consent is required and you do not give it, the app does not request ads. You can review or change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy Options. If that option does not apply in your region, the app tells you so.

Personalized and non-personalized ads

Depending on your region and choices, ads may be personalized using your Advertising ID or may be non-personalized. You can also control this on your device:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → delete your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization (menu names vary by device).
  • Or visit Google My Ad Center.

Ad content

We limit the ad content level in AdMob to “Teens” and block mature-audience ads. We cannot control every individual ad, and we do not endorse any advertiser. If you see an ad that seems inappropriate, you can report it from the ad itself.

If you click an ad, you leave the app and the advertiser’s own privacy practices apply.

5. Storage, backup and deletion

  • Where: your match data is in the app’s private storage on your device.
  • How long: until you delete it. We have no copy, so we cannot recover it for you.
  • Delete one match: Match History → delete icon on that match.
  • Delete everything: Settings → Clear Match History.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its local data from your device.

Device backup. Android’s automatic backup may be turned on for this app. If you have backup enabled in your Google account settings, your device may include the app’s data in your own backup, which is stored and controlled by Google and by you. We do not have access to it. You can switch backup off in your device settings.

6. App permissions

  • INTERNET: used only so the ad and consent software can load ads and the consent form.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: lets the ad software check whether a connection is available.
  • AD_ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): lets the ad software read your Advertising ID for ads and ad measurement. It is not used for anything else.

The app does not ask for camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, location, phone, SMS or storage permissions, and it does not access your photos or files.

7. Features you start yourself

  • Share: when you tap Share on a result or summary, the app creates a short text (team names, scores, result) and hands it to the app you choose, such as a messaging app. What happens next is governed by that app. The app does not send it anywhere itself.
  • Contact / Feedback: opens your email app with a pre-filled address. Whatever you write is sent from your email account, and we use it only to reply to you.
  • Privacy Policy: opens this page in your web browser.

8. What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not use analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools of our own.
  • We do not collect your precise or approximate location ourselves.
  • We do not read your contacts, photos, files or messages.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not send your match data to any server.

9. Security and device checks

Match data stays in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot normally read. We cannot guarantee the security of data on a device that is rooted, modified or compromised.

The app runs simple checks on your device itself, such as whether the device appears to be rooted or an emulator, and whether the app has been tampered with. The result is used only to decide whether to show ads. It is not stored or sent to us or anyone else.

10. Children’s privacy

Cricket Quick Score is intended for users aged 16 and over, and it is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not set up accounts, and the app does not collect personal data for us.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has used the app in a way that concerns you, contact us at mdminhajrahman28@gmail.com. You can also delete all app data from the device with Settings → Clear Match History or by uninstalling the app.

11. Your rights: EEA, UK and Switzerland

Because we do not hold your personal data, there is usually nothing for us to access or erase. For ad-related data, the controller is Google. You have the right, under the GDPR and similar laws, to:

  • access, correct or delete personal data held about you;
  • restrict or object to its processing, including for direct marketing;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing: use Settings → Privacy Options in the app;
  • complain to your local data protection authority.

Where we rely on your consent (for ads and measurement), the legal basis is Article 6(1)(a) GDPR. To exercise rights about data Google holds, use Google’s privacy request form. For anything about this app, write to mdminhajrahman28@gmail.com.

12. Your rights: California and other US states

Under the CCPA/CPRA and similar state laws you may have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion or correction, to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information, and to not be discriminated against for using these rights.

We do not sell personal information. Ads served by Google may use advertising identifiers, which some state laws treat as “sharing” for advertising. You can limit this through the consent and ad controls described in section 4, or by resetting or deleting your Advertising ID in your device settings. You can send requests to mdminhajrahman28@gmail.com. We will tell you if we hold nothing about you.
